# Privacy Policy for Savy

**Last updated:** 18 may 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ABagency ("we", "us", or "our")
collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the Savy mobile
application ("Savy", "the App"). By using Savy, you agree to the practices
described in this policy.

## 1. Who we are

Savy is a personal bookmarking app that lets you save posts and links shared
from social media platforms and the web, and organise them into folders.

## 2. Information we collect

**Account information.** When you create an account, we collect your email
address and a password. Passwords are handled and stored securely by Google
Firebase Authentication; we never see or store your password in plain text.

**Content you save.** When you save a post or link, we store the data
associated with it, including: the original URL, a thumbnail image URL, a
caption, the creator's name or username, tags, and the platform it came from.
We also store any folders you create to organise this content.

**Account usage data.** We store basic account data such as the number of
posts you have saved, your plan type (free or pro), your account creation
date, and your email verification status.

**Technical data.** Our backend provider, Google Firebase, may automatically
process limited technical information (such as IP address, device
identifiers, and diagnostic data) as part of providing authentication and
database services.

We do **not** collect your location, your contacts, or your browsing history
outside the App.

## 3. How we use your information

We use your information to:

- create and secure your account;
- store and display the posts and folders you save;
- enforce free-plan limits and provide account features;
- send you a verification email and account-related messages;
- maintain, troubleshoot, and improve the App.

We do **not** sell your personal information, and we do **not** use it for
advertising.

## 4. Third-party services

Savy relies on **Google Firebase** (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) to
provide account sign-in and to store your data. Your information is processed
and stored on Google's infrastructure and is subject to Google's Privacy
Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

When you save content, Savy fetches publicly available preview data (such as a
thumbnail and caption) from the original link. Opening a saved link, sharing
it, or downloading its media interacts with those third-party services or
websites directly, under their own terms and privacy policies.

## 5. Media downloads

If you choose to download media from a saved post, the App saves that file to
your device's gallery/photos. This requires your permission, and the file
remains only on your device.

## 6. Data storage and security

Your data is stored in Google Cloud Firestore and protected by security rules
that restrict each account's data to that account. While we and Google take
reasonable measures to protect your information, no method of transmission or
electronic storage is completely secure.

## 7. Data retention

We retain your account information and saved content for as long as your
account exists. When you delete your account, this data is removed as
described below.

## 8. Your rights and choices

- **Access:** your saved content is visible to you in the App at any time.
- **Deletion:** you can permanently delete your account from the App's Profile
  screen ("Delete account"). This removes your saved posts, folders, account
  document, and authentication record. This action cannot be undone.
- You may also contact us to request information about the data we hold about
  you.

## 9. Children's privacy

Savy is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the minimum age
required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information
from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal
information, please contact us so we can remove it.

## 10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted at
this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
